### Bug fixes

- The Seestar driver now records each frame's pointing as the plate-solved image centre. It previously used the WCS reference point (`CRVAL1`/`CRVAL2`), which the Seestar puts far from the centre: about 41′ away on a first-light M 31 stack.
- The Seestar driver no longer fails on colour (three-plane) Seestar images whose WCS includes SIP distortion terms.
